Good to know

A few quick answers.

Is this an English grammar class?

No. TalkMates focuses on speaking practice through real conversations. You can receive helpful corrections, but it isn't a structured grammar course.

What if my English isn't very good?

That's okay. If you can understand some basic English and want to get more comfortable speaking, the session can move at your pace. It's most useful for people who know some English already.

What will we talk about?

Whatever interests you — films, food, hobbies, daily life, travel, work, stories, or anything else you're comfortable sharing.

Will you correct my mistakes?

Yes, gently. The focus is on helping you keep talking, not interrupting every sentence. You'll get practical suggestions during or at the end of the session.

Is it one-on-one or a group?

Your introductory conversation is private and one-on-one with a speaking partner.
